Each line starts at a random point and takes small steps. At every step it
turns to face the angle that a simplex noise field gives at that spot. Nearby
points get similar angles, so neighbouring lines run together like currents.

**Turbulence** sets how far the noise can turn a line. Low values give long,
gentle currents:

High values curl the lines into whorls:
