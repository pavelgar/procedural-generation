---
title: Island terrain
description: A low-poly island from layered noise, rendered with three.js.
date: 2026-10-21
tags: [noise, 3d, three.js]
---

The height of each vertex comes from fractal noise: several octaves of simplex
noise stacked at rising frequencies and falling amplitudes. **Octaves** adds
finer detail. **Persistence** sets how much each finer layer counts.

On its own that gives endless rolling hills. To make an island, the height is
pulled down by the square of the distance from the center, so the edges sink
under the **water level** and the middle rises out of it. Everything below the
water is flattened and tinted by depth. Land is colored in bands by height
above the shore.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
